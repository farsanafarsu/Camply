How to connect a website form

Set the form's action to the client's URL, with method POST. Camply reads these field names; only email or phone is required:

name, email, phone, city, message, consent, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term

Add a hidden redirect field with your thank-you page (https://…) to send visitors there after submitting. JSON posts work too. Meta and Google lead-form sync arrives with the live platform connectors.
